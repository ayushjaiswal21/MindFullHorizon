What is stored

Your profile (name, email, college), and the entries you create: check-ins, screening results, journal entries, voice logs, goals, medication reminders, wellness exercise logs, appointments and your points and streak. If you use the crisis support features, we keep a short record of the time and type of the alert, not your words.

Counsellors you have booked with can see your records. Their session notes are theirs and are not part of your download. Access to your records by counsellors is logged.
