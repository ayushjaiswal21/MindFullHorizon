How this differs from other options

Six things this service does, stated as plainly as we can. Where an outside fact helps, it sits on a "Compared with" line with its source below.

Counsellor alert with timed escalation
If a screening answer, journal entry or chat message shows signs of self-harm, an alert appears on the dashboard of your institution's counsellors. If nobody acknowledges it, counsellors are notified again after 15, 30 and 45 minutes (the default). At the second and third step an email also goes to an address the operator has set, but only if the operator has set one. It needs at least one counsellor account at your institution.
Compared with: the maker of one AI companion in a meditation app states that it is "not monitored in real time by a human".
Crisis help does not depend on the AI
A fixed crisis check runs on every chat message before any AI model is asked. If it finds language about harming yourself, you see a fixed reply and these helplines, even if the AI is down: {% for h in helplines %}{{ h.name.split(' (')[0] }} {{ h.number }}{{ ', ' if not loop.last else '.' }}{% endfor %}
Compared with: the maker of one AI companion in a meditation app offers it only in the US, UK, Canada and Australia and points its US and Canadian users to a crisis line in those countries.
PHQ-9 and GAD-7 scored on the server
Totals and score bands are calculated on our server, and a score sent by your browser is ignored. Any answer above zero on PHQ-9 question 9, or a PHQ-9 total of 20 or more, shows help numbers when you submit. The question 9 rule is our own design choice. A screening is not a diagnosis.
Counsellors see linked students only
A counsellor sees an individual student only when the student is linked to them, for example by booking. Institution figures are totals, and any group smaller than 5 is hidden. One exception: open crisis alerts of an institution stay visible to its counsellors, and each such view is logged.
Export and delete your data
From your account you can download the data stored about you and delete your account. {% if session.get('user_id') %}Open your data page.{% else %}You need to be logged in to use this.{% endif %}
A labelled AI that does not diagnose

The companion tells you it is an AI. It is instructed not to diagnose or name medicines or doses, and a filter replaces replies that state a diagnosis or a dose. It does not replace a counsellor, and it does not prescribe: counselling and prescribing are done by people.
{% else %}
With the current settings the companion is labelled an automated assistant: chat replies come from a fixed safety check and saved answers, and no AI model reads what you write. The AI model is used only for general content such as the tip of the day, which is labelled and is instructed not to diagnose or name medicines or doses, with a filter that replaces text stating a diagnosis or a dose. None of this replaces a counsellor, and nothing here prescribes: counselling and prescribing are done by people.
{% endif %}
Compared with: India's Telemedicine Practice Guidelines (2020 text, clause 5.4) do not allow AI platforms to counsel patients or prescribe medicines. That is why we describe the companion as a chat tool, not a counsellor.
