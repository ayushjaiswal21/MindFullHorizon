Date range and how to read this report

  • Mood and digital wellness show the last 90 days of entries. Screening scores include every result on record, and the history table lists the 20 most recent.
  • PHQ-9 (0 to 27) and GAD-7 (0 to 21) are screening questionnaires about the past two weeks. Higher scores mean more symptoms. A score is not a diagnosis, so confirm it in a clinical conversation.
  • Mood is the student's own 1 to 10 rating. Screen time and academic score are entered by the student.
