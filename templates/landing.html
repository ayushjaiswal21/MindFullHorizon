What you can do

Everything below is in the app today. You need an account to use it.

  • {{ icon('mood') }}

    Log your mood

    Rate how you feel from 1 to 10, add a short note, and see your history over time.

  • {{ icon('chart') }}

    Take PHQ-9 and GAD-7 screenings

    Two standard questionnaires for low mood and anxiety. The score is calculated on the server and shown with what it means.

  • {{ icon('chat') }}{% if ai_personal_text %}

    Talk to the AI companion

    Anya is an AI assistant, not a therapist. It listens, suggests coping steps, and points you to a counsellor when it matters.

    {% else %}

    Chat with Anya

    Anya is an automated assistant, not a therapist. It answers from saved answers and a safety check, and no AI model reads what you write. It shows help numbers and points you to a counsellor when it matters.

    {% endif %}
  • {{ icon('journal') }}

    Keep a journal

    Write entries in private and look back at them when you want to.

  • {{ icon('wind') }}

    Breathing, yoga and music

    Guided breathing exercises, yoga sessions and calming audio for study breaks.

  • {{ icon('shield') }}

    Write a safety plan

    A short private plan for hard moments, with the national helplines on it. You can print it, and you choose whether your counsellor can read it.

  • {{ icon('leaf') }}

    Grounding and sleep log

    A five-step grounding exercise that uses your senses, and a simple sleep log with a 14-day chart. Both are optional and private.

  • {{ icon('target') }}

    Set goals

    Track small, measurable goals such as screen time or exercise.

  • {{ icon('calendar') }}

    Book a counsellor

    Request an appointment with a counsellor at your institution and see its status.

  • {{ icon('users') }}

    Counsellor dashboard

    Counsellors see their own patients, appointment requests and screening results in one place.

How it works

Three steps. You decide how often.

  1. Create an account

    Sign up with your email and your college name. You confirm that you are 18 or over.

  2. Check in

    Log your mood, take a screening, or write in your journal. Use the breathing and yoga tools when you need a break.

  3. Get support

    {% if ai_personal_text %}Chat with the AI companion any time{% else %}Use the chat for saved answers and help numbers any time{% endif %}, or book your college counsellor for a real conversation.

For counsellors

Your dashboard is built around the patients who have booked with you. Counsellor accounts are created with an invite code from your institution.

  • {{ icon('users') }}
    Assigned patients only.You see an individual patient's mood, screening results and notes only when they are linked to you, for example by booking an appointment.
  • {{ icon('calendar') }}
    Appointment requests in one list.Accept or decline requests and see today's sessions. An accepted appointment sends you and the student a reminder 24 hours before.
  • {{ icon('check') }}
    Follow-ups after a crisis alert.Acknowledging an alert opens a plan of check-ins, three by default, due 2, 7 and 14 days later. Due and overdue ones show on your dashboard. A flag shows when a student's PHQ-9 or GAD-7 result rises; it is a screening result, not a diagnosis.
  • {{ icon('chart') }}
    Aggregate data for the institution.Institution-level views show combined figures, not individual students.
