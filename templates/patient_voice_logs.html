What is saved

  • Not stored: the audio itself. Your recording is analysed when you press Save and then discarded, so it cannot be played back later.
  • Measurements taken from the sound: length, pitch, loudness. There is no transcript of your words.
  • A rough label for your tone, such as calm or stressed. It is a guess from the sound of your voice, it can be wrong, and it is not a diagnosis.
  • If the AI service is switched on for this site, the measurements (not the audio) may be sent to it to write the short note shown with each log.

Deleting a log removes its measurements and note.
