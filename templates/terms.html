What the service is

Mindful Horizon is a web app for college students in India. It lets you log your mood, take PHQ-9 and GAD-7 screening questionnaires, keep a journal, use wellness exercises, chat with an AI companion and book sessions with a counsellor at your institution. Counsellors use it to see their assigned students, appointments and crisis alerts. It is run by {{ fiduciary_name or placeholder }} as an early-stage project.

Not medical advice or an emergency service

The service gives screening results and general wellness information. It does not give a diagnosis, treatment or medical advice, and it does not replace a doctor, psychiatrist or counsellor. A screening score only shows that you may want to talk to someone.

It is not an emergency service and nobody watches the app around the clock. If you or someone else is in danger, call one of these numbers:

{{ helpline_list(helplines) }}

Who can use it

You must be 18 or older. By creating an account you confirm that you are. If you are younger, do not use the service.

Accounts and invite codes

Student accounts can be created by anyone who meets the age rule. Counsellor accounts need an invite code from the institution. Do not share your password or an invite code. Give accurate details, and tell us if you think someone else has used your account. You are responsible for what happens under your account.

Acceptable use

Do not:

  • use the service to harass, threaten or harm anyone, or to post content that is unlawful;
  • try to get into another person's account or data, or probe the service for weaknesses without our written permission;
  • overload the service, scrape it, or bypass its rate limits;
  • pretend to be a counsellor or another person;
  • use the AI companion to get content meant to harm yourself or others.

We can limit or close an account that breaks these rules.
